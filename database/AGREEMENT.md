# ESTATEHUB SOFTWARE LICENCE, DATA PROCESSING AND DECLARATION AGREEMENT (v7)

> **How to read this document.** This is the human-readable template. The text that is actually signed is generated by the
> platform (`onboarding_service.agreement_text`) when the annexes are sealed; it embeds the SHA-256 of Annexes A to D, and each
> signature binds the hash of that exact text. Clause numbers 1 to 7 and "Execution" below follow the generated text. Where this

## Parties and authority

**The Society** (the "Licensee") — a residential or commercial apartment owners' association, registered society or co-operative
housing society registered under the law that applies to it in its State, acting through its President, Secretary and Treasurer,
who sign below. The Society's name, registration number, PAN and address are taken from its master record.

**The Operator** (the "Licensor") — EstateHub LLP, or the entity named in the Operator's configuration (`OPERATOR_LEGAL_NAME`,
`OPERATOR_ADDRESS`) if that differs, acting through its authorised signatory. The generated text always uses the configured name.

The three office-bearers sign under the election evidence recorded in Annex B (the minutes file and its SHA-256, kept when each was
seated). Whether a Managing Committee resolution or a General Body resolution authorises this contract, and who must sign for the
Operator, is for the advocate to confirm **[advocate to confirm]**.

## 1. Licence and term

The Licensor grants the Licensee a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the EstateHub software, as a hosted service, on
the plan and until the validity date recorded in the Master record, on the fees and renewal terms recorded there. Fees include GST
where it applies. The licence is a contractual permission; the Licensee acquires no ownership of the software, and no intellectual
property is assigned.

This Agreement is a contract under the Indian Contract Act, 1872 and an electronic contract under the Information Technology Act,
2000.

## 2. Society duties

The Licensee is responsible for the accuracy of what is entered in its books, for keeping those books, for appointing its own
auditors, CA and advocate, for the conduct of the advisers it appoints, for keeping owner and role rosters accurate, and for the
accuracy of resident, tenant, vendor and guard details it enters.

The software is provided as is. The Licensor's liability is limited to the extent the law allows.

## 3. Data protection (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)

**Roles.** The Licensee is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data of its owners, tenants, occupants, vendors, guards and staff.
The Licensor is its Data Processor and processes that data only on the Licensee's documented instructions.

**The Licensor** keeps reasonable security safeguards; names each sub-processor to the Licensee (hosting, email and notification
providers, and an eSign vendor where the signing method is eSign), as listed in Annex D; tells the Licensee without undue delay of
any personal-data breach; and on exit returns or deletes the data, subject to retention holds for audits, disputes and statutory
requirements.

**The Licensee** names a grievance officer (shown in Annex D, on the login page and in every notice), publishes a privacy notice,
and answers data-principal requests (access, correction, erasure, grievance, nomination) within the period in its DPDP register.

**Timing.** The Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 apply in phases from their notification. EstateHub applies the notice,
consent, safeguard, breach and retention duties from the first day, ahead of the dates on which some of them become mandatory
**[advocate to confirm the commencement dates]**.

The Society must not collect or store Aadhaar numbers, dates of birth or identity-document scans through onboarding. Evidence files
(election minutes, bank statements, cash-count certificates, review letters, paper signature scans) are kept under their SHA-256 and
are visible only to the Society's administrator, its three office-bearers, its advisers and the Master.

## 4. Key custody

Signing keys are kept per society and per purpose, wrapped under a key held in the host's configuration. Whoever controls the host
and the database can, technically, sign as any society; this is a stated limit of the system, not a promise that it cannot happen.

**The step-up password** is a second password that confirms sensitive society actions: re-issuing or revoking QR gate passes and,
when enabled, rotating or revoking the society's signing keys. It signs nothing and is not a login password. The Licensee holds it
jointly through the President, Secretary and Treasurer. The administrator sets the first one so that setup can finish; within 30 days
of going live it is replaced under the three office-bearers' approvals. It is never stored in readable form. When a core
office-bearer changes, the society is flagged for a step-up password review and the new office-bearers are asked to replace it
within 7 days. A forgotten step-up password cannot be recovered; it is replaced by a three-approval change.

## 5. Liability, indemnity and disputes

As in the Licensor's standard terms. Governing law, the forum for disputes and whether the dispute clause is arbitration (Arbitration
and Conciliation Act, 1996) are settled by the advocate before signing **[advocate to confirm]**.

## 6. Exit

On exit the Licensor exports the Licensee's books, then deletes personal data on a schedule, keeping only what an audit, dispute
hold or law requires. Erasure blanks a person's profile; it does not remove ledger lines the law requires the Society to keep.

## 7. Declared legal basis and opening position (annexes)

Each annex is generated from the Setup Wizard draft, is read-only, and is sealed with its own SHA-256, which is written into the
Agreement text. A failing blocking control prevents sealing. Changing anything after sealing voids the signatures already given.

## 7A. Supplementary annexures

Annexures F (enrollment and undivided-interest basis), G (adoption of Model Bye-Laws 1 to 58) and H (advisers appointed) are added
by the Society alone after go-live and do not require the Operator's signature. Each is chained to this Agreement by its SHA-256 and
is signed by the President, Secretary and Treasurer using the society's chosen signing method. The Operator is informed but is not a
party to these supplementary annexures.

## Execution

**For the Society:** the President, Secretary and Treasurer, each named with flat and the signature image on record (Annex B).
**For the Operator:** its authorised signatory, who signs last.

**Order.** The President, Secretary and Treasurer sign in any order. The Operator signs after all three. Activation follows the
fourth signature.

**Signing method.** The administrator chooses one method for the society before the first signature; it cannot change afterwards.
Every signer also confirms with their own login password.

| Method | What is recorded |
|--------|------------------|
| **Password** (default) | The signer's own login-password confirmation, the SHA-256 of this exact text and the time. This records who confirmed and when; it is not by itself a legal electronic signature. |
| **eSign** | Aadhaar eSign or a Digital Signature Certificate through the eSign provider, with the provider's evidence reference. The Society and the Operator never see or store the Aadhaar number **[advocate to confirm the provider's terms]**. |
| **Paper** | The signer prints, signs and scans this text and uploads the scan. The server computes the scan's SHA-256 and stores it as the evidence reference; nobody types a hash. |

The advocate decides whether password confirmation suffices or whether eSign, a DSC or signed paper is also needed
**[advocate to confirm]**.

**Stamp duty.** Stamp duty on this Agreement is payable under the Indian Stamp Act, 1899 as applied in the Society's State, at the
rate in that State's schedule, with e-stamping where available **[advocate to confirm the amount for the State]**. Evidence of
stamping is recorded with the sealed Agreement.
